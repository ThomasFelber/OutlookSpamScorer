Procedure and documents

How an appointment with the notary works

Every notarial matter has the same four steps. Knowing what comes next and bringing the right documents saves time and follow-up questions.

{{svg:urkunde}}
  1. {{svg:telefon}}Enquiry

    You call or use the contact form. The office asks what the matter is, who is involved and which documents are needed. Then an appointment is arranged.

  2. {{svg:feder}}Draft

    The notary drafts the deed. For contracts with consumers you receive the draft at least two weeks before the appointment so that you can read it at leisure and ask questions.

  3. {{svg:urkunde}}Notarisation

    The notary reads the deed aloud in full; German law requires this. He explains every point; changes are still possible. Then all parties and the notary sign.

  4. {{svg:haken}}Completion

    The office files the deed with the land registry, register court, tax office or municipality, monitors due dates and informs you when everything is complete.

What applies to every appointment

  • Identification. The notary must identify every person by a valid passport or identity card. A driving licence is not sufficient.
  • Anti-money-laundering law. For property and company transactions the notary is obliged to ask for personal details, the beneficial owner and in some cases the origin of the funds. This is routine, not distrust.
  • Language. Deeds are in German. If you do not understand German sufficiently, a sworn interpreter must attend. The office can name interpreters in the region. Most matters can be discussed in English beforehand.
  • Representation. If you cannot attend, you can be represented. The notary explains when a power of attorney must be certified in advance.
  • Reading, hearing, speaking. People who cannot read, hear or speak are notarised too. The law provides special procedures, such as a witness or a sign language interpreter.
{{svg:ausweis}}

Before the appointment

  • Draft read, questions noted
  • Passport or identity card valid
  • German tax identification number for property transactions, if you have one
  • Documents from the checklist at hand
  • Enough time: a notarisation takes 30 to 90 minutes depending on scope

Checklists by matter

What to bring to the appointment. If something is missing, tell the office in advance; much can be obtained.

Property purchase

  • ID, tax ID of both sides
  • Address, land register sheet
  • Purchase price, handover date
  • Financing confirmation
  • For apartments: declaration of division, manager
  • For let property: tenancy agreement

Land charge

  • ID
  • Bank's land charge form
  • Loan amount

Will, inheritance contract

  • ID
  • Marital status, children, grandchildren with dates of birth
  • Overview of assets
  • Earlier dispositions, marriage contract

Power of attorney

  • ID
  • Attorneys with address and date of birth
  • Wishes on medical care and residence

Gift, transfer

  • ID, tax ID of all parties
  • Land register sheet
  • Market value, approximately
  • Wishes on residence, care, reclaim rights

GmbH formation

  • IDs of all shareholders
  • Company name, seat, purpose
  • Share capital and shares
  • Managing directors
  • Beneficial owners

Certificate of inheritance

  • ID
  • Death certificate
  • Family record book, birth and marriage certificates
  • Will, if any
  • Overview of the estate at the date of death

Certification

  • ID
  • Document, unsigned
  • For copies: original
  • Country of use
