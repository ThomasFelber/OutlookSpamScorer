Fees

What the notary costs

Notary fees are fixed by the German Court and Notary Costs Act (GNotKG). The notary may charge neither more nor less. The same deed therefore costs the same at every notary in Germany.

{{svg:waage}}

How the fees arise

The fee depends on the transaction value, for example the purchase price, the assets or the share capital, and on the type of transaction. The notary's time does not matter. A difficult contract costs no more than a simple one of the same value.

The fee includes the draft, advice to all parties, the notarisation and supervision of completion. Expenses for copies, postage and register retrievals plus VAT are added. Fees of the land registry or register court are charged separately.

Advice without notarisation

If advice does not lead to a notarisation, a consultation fee arises, likewise according to the Act and the value. The office tells you in advance.

Cost estimate

Tell the office the matter and the value. You receive an overview of the expected costs before the appointment.

How a fee is calculated

The Act sets a fee rate for each transaction and a table assigning an amount to each transaction value.

TransactionRate
Notarisation of a purchase agreement2.0
Completion, e.g. land register applications0.5
Supervision, e.g. due-date notice0.5
Individual will1.0
Joint will, inheritance contract2.0
Lasting power of attorney1.0
Signature certification0.2

The office tells you the amount for your transaction value before the appointment. Fees of the land registry or register court are added.
