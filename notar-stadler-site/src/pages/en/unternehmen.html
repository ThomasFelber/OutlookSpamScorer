Companies and partnerships

Formation, sale of shares, amendments of the articles and many commercial register filings require a notary in Germany. He makes sure the documents are registered by the court without queries.

Typical matters

  • Forming a GmbH or a UG (haftungsbeschränkt), the German limited company and its low-capital variant
  • Sale or transfer of shares
  • Amendment of the articles, capital increase, change of managing director
  • Commercial register filings for sole traders, partnerships and associations
  • Conversion, merger, business succession
  • Shareholder agreements and succession arrangements

Online notarisation

Forming a GmbH and certain register filings can be done by video via the online platform of the Federal Chamber of Notaries. This requires a German identity card with online function or a recognised EU electronic identity. The office clarifies in advance whether your matter qualifies.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a GmbH formation take?

From the notarisation appointment to registration usually one to three weeks, depending on the register court and how quickly the share capital is paid in and evidenced.

Model protocol or individual articles?

The model protocol is cheaper and sufficient for simple cases with up to three shareholders. With several shareholders with different interests, individual articles are advisable.

Foreign shareholders or managing directors?

Possible without restriction. Foreign documents such as extracts from registers may need an apostille and a translation. The office tells you what is required.

Beneficial owners and the transparency register?

For companies the notary must identify the beneficial owners under anti-money-laundering law. Have the details ready; the entry in the transparency register is the company's responsibility.

What to bring

  • Passport or identity card of all parties
  • Company name, seat, business purpose
  • Shareholders with their shares, managing directors
  • For existing companies: current register extract, list of shareholders, articles
  • Details of the beneficial owners
