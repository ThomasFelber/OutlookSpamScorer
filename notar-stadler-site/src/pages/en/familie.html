Marriage contract, separation, adoption

Many arrangements between spouses and within the family are only valid with notarisation. The notary advises both sides and makes sure that neither is unfairly disadvantaged.

{{svg:ringe}}

Typical matters

  • Marriage contract before or during marriage: separation of property, modified community of accrued gains, protecting a business
  • Separation and divorce: agreement on assets, maintenance, the home, pension rights
  • Adoption of a child, including step-child adoption
  • Acknowledgement of paternity and declaration of joint custody
  • Maintenance agreements that are to be enforceable
  • Agreements between unmarried partners

What the notary does

He clarifies the goals of both sides, drafts the agreement and explains what would apply without a contract. A notarial divorce settlement usually makes the court proceedings shorter and cheaper.

Frequently asked questions

Do we both need a lawyer?

Not for the notarisation. The notary advises both impartially. Anyone who wants their own representation can instruct a lawyer in addition.

Can a marriage contract be changed later?

Yes, at any time by mutual agreement, again by notarisation.

German-Swiss or international marriage?

Then it has to be clarified which matrimonial property law applies. A choice of law in the marriage contract creates certainty.
