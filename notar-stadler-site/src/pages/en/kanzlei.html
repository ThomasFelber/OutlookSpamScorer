Office

Notary's office in Bad Säckingen

Notary Kai-Christoph Stadler holds his seat in Bad Säckingen. The office handles all matters regardless of where the parties live.

{{svg:urkunde}}

The notary

Kai-Christoph Stadler, notary. Holder of a public office, independent, impartial and bound to confidentiality. He may not refuse an official act without good reason.

Career

  • 2007–2013Law studies in Konstanz and Freiburg, first state examination
  • 2013–2015Legal clerkship at Freiburg Regional Court, alongside work at a Freiburg notary's office, second state examination
  • 2015–2017State notary and probate judge in Furtwangen and Donaueschingen, appointed Justizrat in 2017
  • since 2018Notary in full-time office with seat in Bad Säckingen

Deputies

When the notary is absent, the appointed deputy notaries conduct official business: Hans-Hermann Stadler, retired senior judicial officer, and Isabel Weber, qualified lawyer.

The team

Twelve staff in deed preparation, case handling and reception. Every file has a fixed contact person.
