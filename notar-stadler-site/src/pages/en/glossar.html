Glossary

German notarial terms, explained

These words appear in deeds and conversations. At the appointment the notary explains them again. Alphabetical by the German term.

{{svg:buch}}
Abschrift, beglaubigte (certified copy)
A copy whose conformity with the original is confirmed by the notary.
Amtsgericht (local court)
Keeps the land register, the commercial register and handles probate. For the Bad Säckingen district: land registry at Villingen-Schwenningen Local Court, commercial register at Freiburg Local Court.
Auflassung (conveyance)
The agreement of seller and buyer that ownership of the land shall pass. It is part of the purchase agreement and only registered after the purchase price has been paid.
Ausfertigung (official copy)
An official copy of the deed that replaces the original in legal transactions. The original stays with the notary.
Beglaubigung (certification)
Confirmation that a signature is genuine or a copy matches the original. The notary does not check the content.
Beurkundung (notarisation)
The notary draws up the deed, reads it aloud, explains it and signs it together with the parties. He is responsible for its content.
Betreuung (court-appointed guardianship)
Court-ordered representation for people who can no longer manage their affairs. A lasting power of attorney usually makes it unnecessary.
Erbschein (certificate of inheritance)
Certificate of the probate court stating who the heir is. With a notarial will it is usually not needed.
Erbvertrag (inheritance contract)
Contract on succession that binds both sides and can only be changed jointly.
Fälligkeit (due date)
The point from which the purchase price must be paid. The notary gives notice once all conditions are met.
Geschäftswert (transaction value)
The value on which notary fees are calculated: purchase price, assets, share capital.
Grundbuch (land register)
The official register of all land with owners, rights and encumbrances. Only the person entered in the land register is the owner.
Grundschuld (land charge)
The bank's right in the land as security for a loan. Registered in the land register and deleted after repayment. The German equivalent of a mortgage.
Gütertrennung, Zugewinngemeinschaft (matrimonial property regimes)
Rules on how assets are allocated in marriage and equalised on divorce. Without a marriage contract the community of accrued gains applies.
Nießbrauch (usufruct)
The right to use a thing and keep its proceeds, for example to live in a transferred house or let it.
Pflichtteil (compulsory portion)
Minimum share of the estate in money for children, spouses and in some cases parents, even if not named in the will. It amounts to half of the statutory share.
Schlusserbe (final heir)
Person who inherits after the death of the surviving spouse, often the children in a joint will.
Vollzug (completion)
All steps after notarisation until the land register or commercial register shows the new position.
Vormerkung (priority notice)
Entry in the land register that protects the buyer until registered as owner.
Vorsorgevollmacht (lasting power of attorney)
Power of attorney for the case that you can no longer act yourself.
Wirtschaftlich Berechtigter (beneficial owner)
The natural person behind a company. The notary must identify them under anti-money-laundering law.
Wohnrecht (right of residence)
The right, registered in the land register, to live in a house or apartment even though it belongs to someone else.
