Lasting power of attorney, living will, guardianship directive

In Germany, spouses and children may not automatically decide for you if you can no longer do so yourself. Without a power of attorney the court appoints a guardian. With a lasting power of attorney you decide who acts.

{{svg:schirm}}

The three documents

  • Lasting power of attorney (Vorsorgevollmacht): a person you trust may act for you with banks, authorities, doctors, care homes and in property matters.
  • Living will (Patientenverfügung): you set out which medical measures you want or refuse if you can no longer express yourself.
  • Guardianship directive (Betreuungsverfügung): in case a court-appointed guardian becomes necessary after all, you determine who it should be.

Why notarial?

Banks and land registries require a notarial or certified power of attorney for many transactions. The notary also verifies that you have legal capacity and act voluntarily. That protects the document from later doubts. It is registered in the Central Register of Powers of Attorney of the Federal Chamber of Notaries so that courts can find it.

Frequently asked questions

From when is the power effective?

Usually immediately, so that it can be used in an emergency without proof. Internally you agree with your attorney that it is only used when you are no longer able to act. The notary explains the variants.

Can I appoint several people?

Yes, acting individually or only jointly. A main attorney and a substitute is common.

Can I revoke it?

At any time while you have legal capacity. The certified copy is then recalled and the register informed.

Is a foreign power of attorney recognised in Germany?

Often only with difficulty. Banks and land registries expect German form. A German notarial power of attorney avoids delays.
