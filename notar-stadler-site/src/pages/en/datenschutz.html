Contact form and email

Your details are transmitted in encrypted form and used solely to handle your enquiry (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). They are deleted once the enquiry is settled, unless an official act follows; then the retention periods of notarial law apply.

Questionnaires

The questionnaires for preparing an appointment are transmitted in encrypted form and stored on the notary's server in such a way that only the notary's office can read them with its key. The notification to the office contains only the type of questionnaire, the time and a reference number. After retrieval by the office the data are deleted from the server. The details are used solely to prepare a draft (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, section 1 BNotO). The server logs IP address, time and type of questionnaire for secure operation.

Data in official business

For notarisations and certifications the notary processes personal data on the basis of the Federal Notarial Code, the Notarisation Act and the Anti-Money-Laundering Act. This processing is required by law. Detailed information is available at the appointment or on request.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, insofar as statutory retention obligations do not prevent this, and the right to object. Complaints may be addressed to the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg.

External links

Links to OpenStreetMap, the Chamber of Notaries and the Federal Chamber of Notaries lead to external sites. Data are only transmitted to their operators when you click.
