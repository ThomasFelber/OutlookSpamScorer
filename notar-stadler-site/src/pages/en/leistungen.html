What a notary does

The notary acts where the law requires notarial form or where the parties want legal certainty. He advises all sides equally and may not favour anyone.

Also in disputes. The notary can act as a neutral body in disagreements, for example among heirs or shareholders. An agreement before the notary is usually faster than court proceedings and can be made enforceable.
