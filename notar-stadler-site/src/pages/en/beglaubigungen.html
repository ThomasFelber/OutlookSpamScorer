Certifications and documents for abroad

A certification confirms that a signature is genuine or that a copy matches the original. It is quicker and cheaper than a notarisation and often sufficient.

{{svg:apostille}}

Typical matters

  • Signature on a power of attorney, commercial register filing or land register declaration
  • Certified copies of certificates, deeds, passports
  • Declarations for Swiss authorities, banks or land registries
  • Documents to be used abroad that need an apostille

Switzerland

Bad Säckingen borders the canton of Aargau. German notarial documents are recognised in Switzerland when provided with an apostille. The apostille is issued by the President of the Regional Court of Waldshut-Tiengen; the office can obtain it for you. Conversely, we advise on which form Swiss bodies expect for a particular document.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment?

For a signature certification a short arrangement with the office is usually enough. Bring the document unsigned; the signature is made in front of the notary.

What does a certification cost?

Fixed by law: for signatures depending on the transaction value with an upper limit, for copies by number of pages. The office tells you the amount in advance.

Documents in English or another language?

The notary certifies signatures under foreign-language texts as well, provided he understands the content sufficiently. Otherwise a translation is required.
